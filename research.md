## Research interests

My research focuses on learning controllers that enable quadruped robots to move across challenging terrain.

- **Legged robot locomotion:** movement and control of quadruped robots.
- **Reinforcement learning:** learning robot control policies through interaction.
- **Simulation-based training:** developing deep reinforcement learning controllers using Isaac Gym and RSL-RL.
