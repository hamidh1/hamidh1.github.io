I am a Ph.D. researcher at the [University of Science and Technology of China (USTC)](https://en.ustc.edu.cn/), affiliated with the **Intelligent Control and Robotics Laboratory (ICR)** in the **School of Information Science and Technology**. My research focuses on **learning-based locomotion for quadruped robots**, with particular emphasis on **reinforcement learning, robust locomotion, and adaptive navigation across challenging terrains**.
