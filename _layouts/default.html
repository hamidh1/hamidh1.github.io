Hamid Hussain

I am a PhD student at the University of Science and Technology of China, in the Intelligent Control and Robotics Lab (ICR) at the School of Information Science and Technology. I work on locomotion learning of quadruped robots. My PhD advisor is Prof. Kan Zhen.

I hold an MS in Control Science and Engineering from Harbin Institute of Technology, and a BS in Electronic Engineering from Iqra University.

Email  /  GitHub  /  LinkedIn

Research

My research interests include legged robot locomotion, risk-aware reinforcement learning, deep reinforcement learning, and control systems. I aim to build safe, agile, and intelligent quadruped robots that can traverse diverse and challenging terrains.
